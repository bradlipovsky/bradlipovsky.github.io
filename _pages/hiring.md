<h2>What to include when you get in touch</h2>

Keep an introductory message concise. Explain the scientific problem that interests you, why this group is a good intellectual fit, and the experience or perspective you would bring. Include a CV and, where useful, a short example of research, code, writing, or technical work.

The group does not expect applicants to have followed a single conventional path. Clear motivation, curiosity, evidence of sustained effort, and the ability to work constructively with others matter.

<h2>How the group works</h2>

The group’s culture is built around two commitments: act kindly and professionally, and do good work. In practice, that includes:

- a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free research environment;
- early and continuing conversations about authorship and credit;
- open-access publication and reproducible code and data;
- intellectual ownership for students and postdoctoral scholars;
- realistic goals and a sustainable approach to work;
- regular feedback, professional development, and support for varied careers.
