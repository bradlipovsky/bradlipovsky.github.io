The aim is not only to teach techniques, but to help students move confidently between governing physics, observations, computation, and the limits of what is known.

Current teaching at UW spans undergraduate Earth systems and hydrogeology through upper-level and graduate mechanics and glaciology.

UW · 2025 and 2026

ESS 454 · Hydrogeology

Groundwater flow and transport through the lens of physical principles, geological structure, observations, and environmental applications.

UW · 2021, 2023, and 2025

ESS 411/511 · Continuum Mechanics

Stress, strain, constitutive behavior, and conservation laws for Earth materials, with computation and applications across geophysics.

UW · 2024

ESS 431 · Glaciology

The mechanics and climate context of glaciers and ice sheets, from mass balance and flow to sliding, fracture, and ice–ocean interaction.

UW · 2022 and 2023

ESS 107 · Introduction to the Cryosphere

An accessible introduction to Earth’s frozen environments, their role in the climate system, and their rapid contemporary change.

Harvard · 2019

EPS 268 · Machine Learning Across Earth and Planetary Sciences

A research seminar connecting modern data methods to scientific interpretation, reproducibility, and problems throughout Earth and planetary science.
